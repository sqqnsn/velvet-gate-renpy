# Velvet Gate expanded estate

Domine approved this layout and cohort placement for the expanded estate. The estate covers about **10 hectares**. The working site envelope is **400 m east–west by 250 m north–south**. Room sizes, exact doors, and bed capacities are planning allowances.

The expanded estate and the seven-day *Seven Lanterns* demo use the same cast and starting cohort ranks. The promotion awarded in a playthrough belongs to the demo. The AD 33 financial-crisis campaign is a separate continuity. [Cast index](CAST_INDEX.md) · [Seven Lanterns design notes](../DESIGN_NOTES.md)

## Admission and residence

The whole estate is members-only. Livia's formal reception grants **provisional House membership at Tier 0** on the expanded estate. Applicants awaiting reception meet the House off-estate. Once received, a Tier 0 member can use shared courts, gardens, paths, beach, coastline, and the coastal pool. Tier 1 is the first initiated rank and the first promotion moves her into the Lower House.

## Circulation and access

- **Shared routes.** One path from the Lower House court reaches the palace's west gallery. Another descends through the gardens to the promenade, where it joins the palace's seaward route. Members can reach the sand and the southeast pool terrace along the shared paths. The Outer Chamber is the shared network of courts, galleries, and walks.
- **Work routes.** Run a screened rear path from the north yard to the palace service wing and Lower House service door, with a crossing before the palace entrance. Connect the service wing to the west baths and linen facilities. A rear east branch reaches the kitchen for transfers. The kitchen serves summer dining directly.
- **Private access.** Domine's suite, Livia's quarters, and principal Inner House chambers have their own thresholds. The **Inner Chamber reserve** is the separate **12 × 12 m** screened garden, shaded alcove, and approach beside Domine's suite. Ordinary routes bypass it. A lower-tier reward can consist of the visit itself.

## Build order

Put both occupied Lower House halls and Claire's suite in the first build, along with the palace core, shared coastal routes, and work and utility connections. Reserve the Outer House plot for the next intake and fit out the Tier 3 sleeping rooms when duties call for them. Hold space for additional communal buildings. Tier 4–5 housing, final bed capacities and door locations, and a coastal pavilion are open design decisions.
