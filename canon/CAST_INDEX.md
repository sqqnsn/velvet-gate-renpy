## How to use this index

- When writing an individual scene, read the linked profile.
- When writing pairs or groups, also read [Relationships](RELATIONSHIPS.md).
- **Confirmed canon** on individual sheets is supported by character pages or explicit conversation corrections. Sample dialogue and suggested daily-life moments are **proposals** for the game.
- Do not inflate a practical duty (First Mat / Door Girl) into a whole personality or a full office.
